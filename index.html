About Me

My name is Manoj K S, and I am from Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh. I work as a software engineer, and I have always been someone who enjoys exploring ideas, understanding how things work, and finding simple ways to make learning more meaningful. Along with my professional role, I have a strong passion for creating unique, hands-on learning tools for students. I am drawn to developing simple, interactive, and practical learning experiences that make education both engaging and joyful.

For nearly a decade, I was part of The Satsang Foundation Educational Institute, where I actively engaged in providing hands-on education to students. This continues to be my area of deep interest—designing tools, activities, and learning experiences that spark curiosity and help students learn through exploration and doing.

Personal Initiatives

I am currently working on an initiative focused on promoting hands-on education and practical learning approaches for students, particularly in rural India. As part of this initiative, I am working with Rishi Valley School, founded by J. Krishnamurti, exploring ways to create meaningful learning experiences through practical work, making, experimentation, and self-directed learning.

Inspiration

The teachings of J. Krishnamurti and Sri M have been a steady source of inspiration in my life, shaping the way I understand learning, relationships, and inner clarity.
